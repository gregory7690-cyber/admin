How Promotions Work

Chapter Discount: Changes the chapter coin price while the promotion is active.

Featured Book: Places your selected book at the exact dashboard sections you choose.

Free Reading: Marks the selected book for a free-reading campaign.

Promotions automatically become active at the selected start time and disappear from promoted placements after the end time.
